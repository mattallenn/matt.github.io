Personal Portfolio

Projects

POLARIS — ACTIVELY STABILIZED LAUNCHPAD

Lead Software & Electronics Engineer at Purdue Orbital. Developing a cold-gas thruster system to actively stabilize a rocket launchpad before launch. Developed custom PCB, wrote control software, and more!

CHESSMATE — ROBOTIC CHESS OPPONENT

Robotic chessboard opponent built off of an old 3D printer. Designed custom pieces and software to play against Stockfish chess engine. Hoping to add online gameplay in the future!

CUSTOM MECHANICAL KEYBOARD

Fully custom split ortholinear mechanical keyboard with 3D-printed cases, hand-soldered switch matrices, and custom firmware built on Arduino Nano and QMK.

STOMPR — MIDI CONTROLLER

MIDI foot controller built to control digital guitar effects, synths, loopers, etc. Custom PCB, USB MIDI firmware, 3D-printed parts, and more.

SHIMMER — FLIGHT CONTROLLER PCB

Custom STM32-based flight controller designed to stabilize RC planes and drones. Built in KiCad, it receives controller inputs, processes sensor data for stabilization, and outputs PWM to servos or ESCs.

DRUM RISER

Custom drum riser to dampen sound, limit vibrations, and provide a stable platform for my e-kit. Constructed from plywood and tennis balls for under $50.
